# Connect your OpenSubtitles account

Local SRT files work without any online setup. To find and download captions, bring your own OpenSubtitles API key.

1. Create or sign in to your account at [OpenSubtitles.com](https://www.opensubtitles.com).
2. Open your profile and choose **API consumers**.
3. Create a consumer with a unique alphanumeric name, such as `KaptusYourName`.
4. Add a description such as "My personal Kaptus caption companion on iPhone."
5. Enable **Allow anonymous downloads** if you want to download without account credentials and that option is available.
6. Save the consumer and copy its API key.
7. In Kaptus Settings, paste the key into **OpenSubtitles API key** and tap **Done**.
8. If your consumer requires authentication, enter your username and password too. Enter both account fields or leave both empty.

Keep the key private. Do not post it in issues or screenshots. Kaptus stores it in the device-only iPhone Keychain.

**Watch now** requests the best track. **Prepare for theater** requests up to three, with the maximum cost shown before you start. Saved tracks are reused. OpenSubtitles controls your allowance and account restrictions.

Follow the provider's current terms and consumer requirements. See the [official getting-started guide](https://opensubtitles.stoplight.io/docs/opensubtitles-api/e3750fd63a100-getting-started). To skip online setup, choose **Open SRT file** and select a caption file in Files.
