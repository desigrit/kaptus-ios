# Native, quiet, unmistakably Kaptus

The yellow Wordbird remains the brand. It appears in onboarding, the home introduction, and the app icon. No ornamental bird in the cinema player.

Use NavigationStack with a large home title, searchable results, inset grouped Forms for settings, sheets for episode selection and reading controls, and a fullScreenCover for the player. Use SF Pro, SF Symbols, system back navigation, and semantic colors outside the player. Avoid invented glass panels. System navigation and sheets inherit the current iOS appearance.

Brand accent: #FFC857. Ink on the yellow action: #171814. Bird background: #176B70. The player uses #000000 with #FFFFFF captions and restrained gray controls. Home supports light and dark appearances. Teal is used for accessible small links in light mode; yellow remains the filled primary action and dark-mode tint.

Body text follows Dynamic Type. Captions use a scalable 30-point semibold default, adjustable 24 to 44. Every action has at least a 44-point target. Status text is accessible without color. The synchronized notice disappears after four seconds. A faint signed timing adjustment stays top leading. Settings sits top trailing and follows the controls visibility. The caption is the only central persistent element.

The player adapts to landscape with compact horizontal controls and safe-area margins, without recreating its session. No haptics, sounds, or background microphone capture. Brightness is restored when the player loses foreground focus or closes.

First viewport: one clear invitation to follow every line, a yellow Find a movie or TV show action, a secondary Files action, and useful History. Signature interaction: the listening status resolves into the caption, then the chrome quietly recedes.
