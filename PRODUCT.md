# Kaptus for iOS

Kaptus is a personal caption companion for people who rely on captions. Choose a film or TV episode, or open an SRT from Files. The app listens briefly, finds the dialogue in the captions on the device, then stops the microphone and follows the movie with a monotonic clock.

Audience: people who are hard of hearing, watching a screen across a room or in a cinema. English speech and English captions for the initial release. iPhone first, iOS 17 and newer.

Tasks: import, search and prepare, find your place, read quietly, adjust timing, resume after switching apps. Captions remain useful offline. There is no cloud transcription and no unknown-movie identification.

User explicitly authorized autonomous platform decisions and a separate public GitHub repository while asleep. Keep the established yellow Wordbird brand. Native iOS navigation and accessibility take precedence over copying Android geometry.
